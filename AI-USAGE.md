# Little Voyages: AI and automated-use policy

Updated October 6, 2026.

Little Voyages is a personal cruise logbook. Public access to the site, repository, or downloads does not grant blanket permission to reuse the owner's personal travel records, original writing, illustrations, or other original content in AI systems.

## Authorized use of exports

The owner may give an AI assistant exported files and explicitly authorize a particular task, such as analyzing the logbook, making a travel visualization, or maintaining this project. Someone acting with the owner's permission may do the same. That permission covers the supplied material and the requested task only.

Keep the source references, uncertainty notes, and distinction between approximate routes and actual sailing tracks. Do not treat historical itinerary estimates as confirmed personal movements. Task-specific permission does not authorize model training, unrelated reuse, resale, public redistribution, or collection of additional personal information. An agent maintaining this project may read and change the material necessary for the owner's authorized work.

## Uses not authorized by this site

Without the owner's explicit permission, do not:

- Harvest, scrape, aggregate, or ingest this site's personal content for AI services, AI search indexes, retrieval databases, or datasets.
- Use the content to train, fine-tune, evaluate, or improve AI or machine-learning models, including image models.
- Sell the content, build personal profiles from it, or redistribute it as a dataset.
- Bypass access controls, exploit vulnerabilities, compromise accounts, alter or delete files, or disrupt the site, repository, visitors, or hosting services.

Normal human browsing, sharing links and their previews, and ordinary search-engine indexing are welcome. Those activities do not grant the AI-use permissions above. Authorized analysis of owner-supplied downloads remains welcome.

This policy states the owner's permissions for content they control. It does not replace applicable law or override the licenses and rights of third-party software, fonts, map data, public source material, or other third-party assets. Retain their attribution and license terms.

## What these files can and cannot do

`robots.txt` is a voluntary crawler convention, not access control. Crawlers normally read it only at the origin root. This project's file is served at `https://dev1niscool.github.io/little-voyages/robots.txt`; it is an advisory copy and template, **not an active origin-level robots exclusion file**. The effective location would be `https://dev1niscool.github.io/robots.txt`. The separate portfolio repository is not changed by this project.

The page also includes `noai` and `noimageai` metadata. These are nonstandard, voluntary signals with limited support. Neither that metadata nor this policy can stop a bot, prevent hacking, guarantee removal from existing datasets, or prevent reuse of publicly accessible files. User-triggered agents may handle robots preferences differently, and the named crawler list is not exhaustive. GitHub controls crawler behavior on `github.com`; a file in this repository cannot configure GitHub's domain.

Choose an AI service's privacy and training settings appropriately before supplying an export. This policy cannot change that service's settings or terms.
